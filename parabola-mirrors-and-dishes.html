CH.01 / 10 — THE ONE CURVE THAT FOCUSES

Every ray travels the same distance to the focus

A parabola is the set of points equally far from the focus and the directrix.

Equal path length is the whole secret.

THE PARABOLA AND ITS FOCUS
y = x² / 4f  ·  F = (0, f)  ·  directrix y = −f  ·  N = f / D
f is the focal length, D the aperture, N the focal ratio.
