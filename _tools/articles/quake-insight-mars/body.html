CH.05 / 09 — CRUST, MANTLE, CORE

A core that keeps shrinking

Crust 20 or 39 km; lithosphere ~500 km [19][20].

2021: 1,830 km; RISE 1,835; SKS 1,780–1,810 [21][22][24].

2023: molten layer over a 1,650–1,675 km core [12][23].

As of November 2026: 1,650–1,675 km; inner core debated [25][26][27].

WHERE THE CORE CASTS ITS SHADOW (STRAIGHT RAYS)
Δshadow = 2 arccos(rc / R)  ·  r sin i / v = const
Straight-ray shadow edge; the real shadow covers half of Mars [21].

CH.06 / 09 — THE MOLE

Five metres planned, thirty-seven centimetres reached

The mole: 400 × 27 mm, aiming for 3–5 m [29][30].

Recoil 5–7 N needs friction; the duricrust gave none [29].

Ended on 9 January 2021 at ~37 cm [29][30].

Soil properties learned; heat flow not measured [29].
