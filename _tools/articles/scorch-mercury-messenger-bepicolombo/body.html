Mariner 10 and Colombo's billiard shot

Mariner 10 used Venus to reach Mercury in 1974.

CH.03 / 09 — SIX FLYBYS AND ONE BURN

MESSENGER: six and a half years of braking

MESSENGER flew past Earth once, Venus twice and Mercury three times.

On 18 March 2011 a 0.86 km/s burn put MESSENGER into orbit.

CH.05 / 09 — THE 3:2 SPIN–ORBIT RESONANCE

A day that lasts two years

Mercury spins three times for every two orbits.

A Mercury day lasts 176 Earth days.

Ice on the planet nearest the Sun
